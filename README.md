## Overview

ClientSynth is a multi-tenant SaaS platform and API for generating coherent, high-dimensional synthetic data using AI. Within the DeeperPoint ecosystem, ClientSynth serves as the core population engine for **Digital Twin market simulations**.

It allows market engineers (using tools like MarketForge) to model and spawn hundreds of synthetic buyers, sellers, and facilitators—complete with generated CVs, project specs, and complex capability profiles—to test a thin market's dynamics before real users enter.

## ⚠️ Strict Usage Restriction: Ethical Boundaries

**Synthetic profiles generated by ClientSynth must never be mixed with real participants, and must never be presented as real.** This is a condition of the [license](LICENSE), not a courtesy.

- **Permitted:** populating a prototype market to test matching mechanics, simulating market friction, and demonstrating a market to sponsors and investors, provided every participant shown is synthetic and identified as such.
- **Prohibited:** putting synthetic users into a live market with real participants, combining synthetic and real records in one pool, presenting synthetic content as real, and removing or bypassing the watermark and provenance marks.

A real-world marketplace (powered by Cosolvent) must start with zero synthetic users. Recruiting real participants is the market sponsor's responsibility. Terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) Part 1; breach terminates the license.

## Relationship to Cosolvent

ClientSynth operates **outside** of the [Cosolvent](https://github.com/DeeperPoint/Cosolvent) matching engine. 
- **Cosolvent** is the live matching engine that processes profiles and forms deals.
- **ClientSynth** generates the mock JSON data conforming to a specific market's ontology, which is then injected into a test instance of Cosolvent to validate that the market's design actually works.
